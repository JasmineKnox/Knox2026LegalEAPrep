About this build

Version 1 is designed for the current QCAA Legal Studies 2025 v1.3 syllabus (January 2026). The current external assessment assesses Objectives 1, 3 and 4 and relates to Unit 4 Topic 1 and Topic 3.

The game uses teacher-provided EA preparation resources, the current QCAA syllabus and the 2025 QCAA external assessment/marking guide as practice models. The Port Calder boss battle is fictional classroom stimulus; it is designed to rehearse legal reasoning, not to state that a fictional Bill or stakeholder exists.

Primary source basis

  • QCAA Legal Studies 2025 v1.3 General senior syllabus (January 2026).
  • QCAA External assessment 2025 question/response book and marking guide.
  • Mrs Knox Week 1–3 EA preparation materials.
  • Mrs Knox treaty, human-rights scrutiny, Commonwealth powers/limits and Chapter III resources.
Accuracy rule: where the game teaches a legal process, it distinguishes the source/actor, legal meaning or effect, and consequence. Fictional stimuli are labelled as fictional.
